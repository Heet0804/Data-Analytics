# 📗 Excel Data Analytics

Four Excel projects, from a simple salary dashboard to a full school performance dashboard. Each project has its own folder with the workbook and a detailed README.

## 📂 Projects at a Glance

| # | Project | What it covers | Main skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Salary Dashboard](Project%20-%201%20Dashboard/README.md) | Data job salaries by title, country and job type | Charts, formulas, data validation |
| 2 | [Data Jobs Analysis](Project%20-%202%20Analysis/README.md) | Skills, pay and regions in the data job market | Power Query, PivotTables, DAX, Power Pivot |
| 3 | [IPL Analysis](Project%20-%203%20IPL%20Analysis/README.md) | What drives wins in 11 IPL seasons (2008 to 2018) | Pivot charts, slicers, XLOOKUP |
| 4 | [School Performance Dashboard](Project%20-%204%20School%20Analysis/README.md) | Marks, attendance and fees of 300 students | Slicers, GETPIVOTDATA, COUNTIFS, AVERAGEIFS |
